## Exact-build checkpoints and history

The materialization and Damage-to-zero committed gzip checkpoints were regenerated by the
checked-in scripts from real Core test factories and public session decisions after installing
the exact lock. Their provenance and SHA256 hashes are recorded beside each fixture under
`tests/fixtures/`. The materialization helper creates an in-progress domain scenario, and the
Damage-to-zero helper begins at an in-progress shooting boundary. Neither checkpoint is a
roster-start traversal or a cross-version save. Old persistence and replay artifacts require
their own original runtime. Contract 43 requires historical target context and new group IDs;
this UI adds no metadata edit, save conversion or cached-request migration.

The measured Contract 42 smoke and startup numbers in the prior M2 record are historical
same-Core/same-runner observations. The intervening Core catalog reuse changes selected tests
and offline geometry generation, not a measured UI startup cache. This adoption adds no UI
cache of all faction JSON. A separate current-Core measurement may report current import,
identity verification, config, traversal, projection and window costs, but it cannot establish
a controlled 42-to-44.1 speedup on its own.
